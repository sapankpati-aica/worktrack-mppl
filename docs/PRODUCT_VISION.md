# Matterhorn One — Product Vision

## Product Name

Matterhorn One

## Positioning

Matterhorn One is a unified enterprise operations platform designed to integrate business workflows, employee management, compliance, finance, procurement, projects, documents, reporting, and AI-assisted operations into a single system.

## Current Origin

Matterhorn One originated from WorkTrack, an internal task and compliance management application developed for operational use.

The current application serves as the functional baseline and prototype for the broader Matterhorn One platform.

## Core Problem

Businesses often operate through disconnected spreadsheets, emails, messaging applications, accounting tools, HR systems, document repositories, and manual approval processes.

This creates:

- duplicated work
- poor accountability
- missed deadlines
- limited management visibility
- fragmented business data
- dependency on individual employees
- weak audit trails

Matterhorn One aims to provide a single operational source of truth.

## Target Users

Matterhorn One is intended for:

- small and medium enterprises
- professional consulting firms
- finance and accounting teams
- project-based businesses
- compliance teams
- interior design and construction firms
- sustainability and ESG advisory businesses
- growing multi-entity organisations

## Core Platform Capabilities

The platform may ultimately include:

- task and workflow management
- employee and HR management
- attendance, leave, and payroll
- finance and accounting workflows
- procurement and vendor management
- compliance tracking
- document management
- CRM and client management
- project management
- approvals
- dashboards and analytics
- notifications
- audit trails
- AI Copilot
- client and vendor portals

## Product Principles

### 1. Operational Simplicity

The platform must remain understandable for non-technical users.

### 2. Modular Architecture

Each business function should operate as an independent module connected through a shared core platform.

### 3. Security by Design

Authentication, permissions, audit trails, backups, and data protection must be treated as core functionality.

### 4. Single Source of Truth

Business information should be recorded once and reused across workflows.

### 5. Automation First

Repeated manual activities should be automated wherever practical.

### 6. AI as an Operational Layer

AI should assist users in retrieving information, preparing documents, analysing business data, and initiating workflows.

### 7. Controlled Development

Production stability must not be compromised by development activities.

## Long-Term Vision

Matterhorn One should evolve into an AI-enabled enterprise operating system where users can manage business functions through both structured modules and natural-language instructions.

Example commands may include:

- Show pending statutory compliances.
- Generate the monthly salary sheet.
- Show vendor invoices overdue by more than 30 days.
- Draft an appointment letter.
- Summarise delayed projects.
- Show tasks pending with each employee.
- Prepare a management dashboard.

## Current Development Strategy

The existing WorkTrack application will remain the functional baseline.

Development will proceed through controlled branches, documentation, backups, testing, and staged releases.

Major architectural restructuring will only be undertaken after the existing application has been documented and stabilised.
